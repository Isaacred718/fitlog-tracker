CSV Sync Info

How to sync with Numbers on iCloud:

  1. Click Export All CSV to download workouts.csv and measurements.csv
  2. Move the CSV files to your iCloud Drive folder
  3. Open them in Numbers — they'll display as spreadsheets
  4. To import data back, use Import CSV above

Data is also saved in your browser's local storage automatically.
